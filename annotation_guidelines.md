Bengali Text Annotation Guidelines

1. Purpose

These guidelines define a simple and consistent approach for annotating Bengali text for AI training and Natural Language Processing (NLP) tasks.

The main goal is to classify Bengali sentences according to their topic and sentiment while preserving the original meaning.

2. Text Preservation

The original Bengali sentence should be preserved whenever possible.

Annotators should not unnecessarily change:

- Words
- Sentence structure
- Meaning
- Spelling

3. Category Annotation

Each sentence should receive the category that best represents its primary topic.

Weather

Sentences related to weather, temperature, rain, clouds, seasons, or atmospheric conditions.

Daily Life

Sentences describing everyday activities and routines.

Language

Sentences related to Bengali language, grammar, spelling, vocabulary, or linguistic concepts.

Book

Sentences related to books, stories, novels, reading, or literature.

Entertainment

Sentences related to movies, music, television, or other entertainment.

Food

Sentences related to food, cooking, meals, taste, or eating.

Education

Sentences related to school, college, examinations, teachers, or formal education.

Learning

Sentences related to acquiring knowledge, practicing skills, or personal learning.

Work

Sentences related to jobs, office activities, professional tasks, or workplace experiences.

Technology

Sentences related to technology, artificial intelligence, software, computers, or digital systems.

4. Sentiment Annotation

Each sentence is assigned one of three sentiment labels.

Positive

Use Positive when the sentence expresses:

- Happiness
- Satisfaction
- Appreciation
- Enjoyment
- Approval
- Positive experience

Example:

"এই বইটি আমার খুব ভালো লেগেছে।"

Sentiment: Positive

Negative

Use Negative when the sentence expresses:

- Dissatisfaction
- Dislike
- Difficulty
- Frustration
- Negative experience

Example:

"এই সিনেমাটি আমার একদম ভালো লাগেনি।"

Sentiment: Negative

Neutral

Use Neutral when the sentence mainly provides information without expressing a clearly positive or negative opinion.

Example:

"আমি গতকাল একটি সিনেমা দেখেছি।"

Sentiment: Neutral

5. Annotation Principles

Annotators should:

1. Read the complete sentence before assigning a label.
2. Consider the meaning and context.
3. Select the category that best represents the primary topic.
4. Avoid making assumptions that are not present in the sentence.
5. Apply the same rules consistently across the dataset.
6. Preserve the original meaning of Bengali text.

6. Quality Control

Before finalizing an annotation, check:

- Is the Bengali sentence clear?
- Is the category appropriate?
- Is the sentiment supported by the sentence?
- Has the original meaning been preserved?
- Has the same annotation rule been applied consistently?

7. Important Note

This dataset is intended as a portfolio and demonstration project for Bengali language annotation and AI training-related skills. It is not presented as a production-ready benchmark dataset.
